Today I decided to see two completely different sides of the city. 
- Morning — Asakusa. 
- Evening — Akihabara.

#### **Senso-ji**

Very touristy. Very. And yet, I liked it. Especially early in the morning, when there aren't quite as many people. At one point I just sat down away from the main flow of people and watched everyone for about twenty minutes. Tourists taking pictures of the temple. Locals going about their business. Someone praying. Someone trying to get their kid to stop screaming. The last one was the most convincing.

#### **Akihabara**

A completely different planet. Advertising, arcades, electronics stores, music, anime, people carrying bags. I'm not a huge anime fan, but I really liked the atmosphere itself. Especially the arcades. I went in for “five minutes.” I came out two hours later. Lost about ¥2,000. Don't regret it. Probably. Little discovery of the day Japanese vending machines are a whole art form of their own. Hot coffee from a vending machine after several hours of walking somehow feels like a gift from the universe.

##### **Day rating: 9/10**
