Day 3 — Kamakura

Today I escaped Tokyo.

About an hour by train and the city already feels completely different.

Fewer people, more greenery, the sea.

Great Buddha

First impression:

“Okay, it's actually huge.”

Photos really don't capture the scale.

But I liked the road leading there even more.

Small shops, wooden houses, bicycles, the smell of food.

Hasedera

This is where I stayed for a while.

I especially liked the garden.

After Tokyo, for the first time I just wanted to stop going anywhere.

I sat on a bench.

Looked at the plants.

Didn't rush.

And realized that for the last two days I had been constantly trying to “see Japan.”

But that's impossible.

By the sea in the evening

I walked down to the beach.

The weather was a little cloudy.

And strangely enough, it was perfect.

No beautiful sunset, no postcard moment, no “wow.”

Just the sea and the wind.

Sometimes those are the moments you remember better than anything else.
